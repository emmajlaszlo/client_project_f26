Wrap up and Next Steps

Reflecting and translating your impact. A handoff is not done until the partner can use what you left them.

Why closing well matters

A project is not finished when you submit the file. The partner still needs a clear handoff, and you still need a way to talk about the work after the course ends.

Ending an Experience with an Organization:

As you conclude your project, it's essential to ensure a smooth transition and handoff of responsibilities. This involves not only delivering final products but also equipping your partner with the knowledge and tools they need to sustain the work.

  • Package code, designs, and reports in a format your partner can open and update.
  • End the relationship with thanks, a clear end date, and no vague promises.
  • Compare what you delivered with the original goals and ask for client feedback.

Preparation for What's Next:

  • Turn project work into resume bullets and interview answers.
  • Document process, decisions, and outcomes in a portfolio.
  • Stay in touch with teammates, mentors, and partners.
